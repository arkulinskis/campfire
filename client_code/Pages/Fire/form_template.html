Sit by the fire.

A real campfire, simulated live: flames from a fluid simulation, crackles made by chance, logs that char, glow and fall in. It has never burned this way before, and never will again.

Best with headphones, in the dark. Drag to look around.

Campfire

This fire needs a little more from your browser.

Campfire draws its flames with WebGL 2, which this browser or device has turned off. Recent Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari all have it; if it is turned off in settings, switching it back on brings the fire.
